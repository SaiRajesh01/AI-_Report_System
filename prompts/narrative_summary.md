# Section: Narrative Summary and Analysis

Write the "Narrative Summary and Analysis" section of a PADER report.

This is the main analytical section. Provide an overall understanding of the safety information received during the reporting period.

## Required content (use ONLY the evidence provided):

1. **Opening**: State the product name, reporting period, and total cases received.
2. **Seriousness overview**: How many cases were serious vs. non-serious. Mention the percentage.
3. **Drug characterization**: State how many cases had the product as suspect vs. concomitant vs. interacting. This is critical context.
4. **Demographics summary**: Summarize sex distribution and age characteristics (mean, median, range). Note missing data.
5. **Geographic distribution**: Summarize the top reporting countries.
6. **Most common reactions**: List the top reported Preferred Terms with counts.
7. **Serious reactions**: Note the most frequent serious reactions specifically.
8. **Outcomes**: Summarize the outcome distribution (recovered, fatal, unknown, etc.).
9. **15-Day Alert cases**: State the number of expedited cases and fatal cases among them.
10. **Notable observations**: If the evidence shows any trend (e.g., volume change over time), state it as an observation, not a conclusion.

## Formatting:
- Use flowing prose paragraphs, not bullet points.
- Include key numbers inline (e.g., "1,024 cases").
- Do NOT create tables in this section — tables belong in the Summary Analysis of Cases.
