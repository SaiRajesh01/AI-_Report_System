# Section: Trends and Important Observations

Write the "Trends and Important Observations" section of a PADER report.

This section presents meaningful observations about patterns in the reporting data.

## Required content (use ONLY the evidence provided):

1. **Case volume over time**: Present the monthly and/or quarterly case counts. State whether volume is stable, increasing, or decreasing based on the trend summary in evidence.

2. **PT temporal patterns**: If any of the top PTs show notable monthly variation, describe it as an observation.

3. **Demographic patterns**: Note any notable concentrations (e.g., predominantly elderly population, geographic concentration).

4. **Other observations**: Any other patterns supported by the evidence.

## CRITICAL RULES for this section:
- Present patterns as OBSERVATIONS, not conclusions.
- A numerical pattern is NOT automatically a safety signal.
- CORRECT: "Reports of Reaction X increased from 12 cases in January to 31 cases in March."
- INCORRECT: "Reaction X represents a confirmed emerging safety signal."
- Let the human reviewer determine significance.
- This is a first reporting period — no comparison to prior periods is possible.
- Do NOT infer causation or make medical judgments.

## Formatting:
- Mix of prose and small tables for temporal data.
- Keep interpretive language cautious and factual.
